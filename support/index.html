Record front and back together

In Video or Dashcam mode, tap the camera switch button and choose both cameras. Tap either camera chip to turn it off again. Choose what gets saved in Settings › Dual Camera.

My music stops when I record

It shouldn't. Ace Cam mixes with other audio and always records with the iPhone microphone, so headphones keep playing music. If music stops, please email us with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Use Ace Cam from the Lock Screen or Camera Control

Open Ace Cam once and allow camera access. Then go to Settings › Camera › Camera Control and choose Ace Cam, or add the Ace Cam control in Control Center, on the Lock Screen or to the Action button.

Captions

Turn on Captions (CC) before recording. After recording, open the clip from the gallery to edit text, timing and style, then export with captions burned in, as a switchable track with an .srt file, or without captions.

Apple Watch

Install Ace Cam on your watch from the Watch app on iPhone. Keep Ace Cam open on your iPhone; tap record or photo on the watch.
