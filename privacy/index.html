Privacy Policy

Ace Cam does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Camera, microphone and photos

Ace Cam uses your cameras and microphone only to take the photos and videos you ask for. Your photos and videos are saved to your own Photos library and to Ace Cam's private storage on your iPhone. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Speech recognition

Captions and voice commands use Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to Apple, to us, or to anyone else. If on-device recognition isn't available for a language, captions are not offered for that language.

Face blur and retouching

Face detection, blurring and retouching are performed entirely on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework. No face data leaves your device and no facial recognition profile is created.

Location

If you turn on location tagging, the location is written into your photos and videos in your library. It is not sent to us.

Apple Watch

The Watch app sends shutter commands directly to your iPhone. Nothing goes through a server.

Children

Ace Cam collects no data from anyone, including children.
